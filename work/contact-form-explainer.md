# Portfolio Contact Form

## What is a backend?

A backend is the part of a website that works behind the scenes. It receives and processes information that the frontend alone cannot handle.

## What does my feature do?

I added a working contact form to my portfolio. Visitors can enter their name, email, and message to contact me.

## How does the data flow?

The visitor fills out the form and clicks **Send Message**. The portfolio sends the form data to Formspree using a POST request. Formspree processes the submission and makes it available to me through its dashboard and email notifications.

## Test

I tested the contact form on the live portfolio with a real submission and confirmed that the message was received successfully.
